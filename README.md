# Awesome-Warranty-Management

## Top Warranty Management Tools Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Product Protection Plans, Warranty Registration, Claims Processing, RMA & Post-Purchase Experience*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Warranty Management**. These tools handle extended warranties and protection plans, product registration, claims intake and adjudication, RMA/repair workflows, expiration tracking, document storage, and analytics for merchants, manufacturers, and service organizations.

**Examples** include Clyde, Extend, Mulberry, Centriq, Upsie, SureBright, ProtectAll, Consumer Priority Service, WarrCloud, and Warranty Master (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, personal or enterprise warranty tracking, claims lifecycle management, and open RMA systems — ideal for merchants, manufacturers, repair shops, IT teams, and developers seeking data ownership and customizable warranty workflows.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Warracker](https://github.com/sassanix/warracker)**  
  Open-source, self-hostable warranty tracker for monitoring expirations, storing receipts and documents, managing claims, multi-user support, and proactive alerts.

- **[Suredly / WarrantyApp](https://github.com/i-am-regs/WarrantyApp)**  
  Modern React/Node.js warranty management system for product registration, serial number lookup, claim submission/tracking, role-based access, and analytics.

- **[WarrantyManager](https://github.com/Aravin/WarrantyManager)**  
  Application for tracking product warranties, purchase details, periods, bills, and related service information for personal or business assets.

- **[mcp-warranty](https://github.com/zavora-ai/mcp-warranty)**  
  Warranty management platform (MCP server) covering products, plans, registrations, entitlement checks, claims lifecycle, RMA/repair, and analytics with full audit trail.

- **[WarrantEase](https://github.com/mikkogozalo/warrantease)**  
  Blockchain-oriented (EOS) open-source system for secure product warranty registration, claims, and extension actions.

- **[Epickod RMS](https://github.com/Epickod-ltd/rms)**  
  Repairing and warranty management system (desktop) with device tracking, warranty records, customer history, and full repair workflows.

- **[Agentic Warranty Claims Processor](https://github.com/raksh-dev/Warranty-Claims-Processor)**  
  Open-source agentic AI system for end-to-end warranty claim processing including email triage, extraction, policy selection, and human-in-the-loop decisions.

- **[GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi)**  
  Comprehensive open-source ITSM and asset management platform that includes contract/warranty tracking, ticketing, and inventory capabilities adaptable to warranty operations.

- **[Snipe-IT](https://github.com/snipe/snipe-it)**  
  Open-source asset management system with strong support for licenses, warranties, depreciation, and related lifecycle tracking.

- **[Community RMA & Return Systems](https://github.com/topics/rma)**  
  Various open-source RMA, return, and warranty request modules (including WooCommerce extensions) for handling post-purchase claims and repairs.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Personal warranty trackers and mobile apps (Warranty Roster, Warranty Wallet style projects).
- ITSM tools such as **iTop**, **Zammad**, or **Request Tracker** that can be configured for warranty/claims workflows.
- Custom agentic or LLM-powered claim triage and policy engines built on open frameworks.
- Spreadsheet + script solutions and CSV-based trackers for lightweight needs.
- Many manufacturer-specific or MSP-oriented warranty lookup scripts (Dell, etc.).

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Start with **Warracker** or **Suredly** for core tracking and claims, integrate **GLPI/Snipe-IT** for asset context, add document storage (Paperless-ngx or MinIO), use **PostgreSQL** for records, expose APIs for registration portals, and layer local LLMs (Ollama) or agent frameworks for automated claim triage, entitlement checks, and natural-language status updates.
